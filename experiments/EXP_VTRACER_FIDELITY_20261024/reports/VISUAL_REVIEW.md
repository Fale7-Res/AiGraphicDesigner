# Visual review — six assigned final cases

Reviewer: vtracer_sources. Actual viewed files: `comparisons_final/<case>/full_100.png` for A02, A04, A06, A07, A08 and A11; additionally A08 `center_400.png` and A11 `center_200.png`. The full sheets place Original, Owner preset, Final selected, Original V04 and Repaired V04 in that order. This is an inspection of source-resolution native SVG renders, not a blinded user study or higher-resolution ground truth. Parent separately reviews the other six cases.

| Case | Direct visual observation | Remaining limitation / selection assessment |
|---|---|---|
| A02 Falafel | Final preserves fine fried surface flecks, cut green interior, plate edge and blurred sauce bowl much more closely than the owner's spline preset. V04 shows obvious blocks in background and bowl; Repair merges food texture into broad patches. | No visible reason found to veto the numerical final candidate at native size. Small pixel-like geometry is responsible for fidelity; no meaningful falafel groups are demonstrated. |
| A04 Fries | Final retains fry highlights, thin edges, ketchup contours and bowl shading close to source. Owner preset has more contour stair-stepping/faceting; V04 visibly blocks the background and portions of food. | Native view supports retention of final selection. This does not demonstrate clean enlarged contours or actual soft-shadow gradients. |
| A06 Tomatoes | Droplets, stems, fruit skin shading and wooden surface in the final are visually very close to source. Owner preset has broken/faceted edges and simplified highlight regions; V04 and Repair have stronger block/region artifacts. | No native-size visual veto. Tiny source-derived colored areas remain numerous; the detail is not a procedural droplet/material representation. |
| A07 Wrapped sandwich | Final preserves bread crumb/toasting texture, lettuce boundaries, filling and wrapper marks closely. Owner preset coarsens bread and filling; V04 has visible rectangular background/food artifacts; Repair removes much fine texture. | No native-size visual veto. Preserved wrapper marks are not recognized editable text, nor are ingredients separated semantically. |
| A08 Latte | Final retains latte-art linework, rim and beans better than other compared candidates. However a flat gray/brown patch with speckled boundary is visible on the front of the cup; at400% the original has a smoother tonal transition. | **Residual defect remains:** broad smooth appearance is flattened despite strong overall fidelity. This is not fixed by counting retained small details. The alternatives shown have worse banding/faceting, so no veto was issued in favor of them; this is not perfect reconstruction acceptance. |
| A11 Cake | Final retains sponge pores, cream lines, chocolate drips, berry detail and soft background closely. At200% the final preserves much more texture than owner preset and Repair; V04 retains detail but adds larger visible blocks. | No native-size visual veto. This fidelity is realized with dense pixel-derived paths; no compact chocolate surface/shadow model is established. |

All six final candidates were retained by this reviewer; retention means they were not visibly worse than the displayed alternatives in the inspected views. It does not certify perfect fidelity, professional editability, all-scale quality or a completed product milestone. The cup defect is explicitly retained as a negative result. The final numerical selections remain separately documented in `finalists.json`; visual review did not silently change their provenance.
